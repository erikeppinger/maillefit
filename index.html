1 · Client measurements

Only a soft tape is needed, so a client can do this alone in front of a mirror. First stick four small dots (tape or a washable marker) on each breast, then measure between the dots over the skin. Stand upright, arms relaxed, tape snug but not pulling. Click a field or a number to see it in the drawing.

    1 · Body measurements

    Soft tape, over whatever will be worn under the mail (a shirt or padded jacket). Girths snug but not pulling. Measure the body as it is — room to move (ease) is added under Construction. Click a field or a number to see it in the drawing.

    3 · Swatch calibration

    Optional, but it makes every count fit your rings. Weave a small swatch with the real rings, lay it flat and smooth it out without stretching. Along a row: rings leaning the same way, side by side. Rows: count row after row across. Measure from the centre of the first ring to the centre of the last.
