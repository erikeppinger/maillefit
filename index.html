1 · Client measurements

Soft tape only — a client can do this alone in front of a mirror. First stick four small dots (tape or washable marker) on each breast, then measure between the dots over the skin. Stand upright, arms relaxed, tape snug but not pulling. Click a field or a number to see it in the drawing.

    3 · Swatch calibration
